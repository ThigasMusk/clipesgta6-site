Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

1. Summary

clipesgta6 runs entirely on the user's own computer. We do not operate servers that receive, store or sell your data.

2. Data we access from TikTok

  • user.info.basic: your TikTok open ID and basic profile information, used only to confirm which account is connected.
  • video.upload: permission to upload videos to your account as drafts. We cannot publish, delete or read your existing videos.

3. Where data is stored

The access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok are saved only in a local file on your computer, used to upload drafts when you run the tool. They are never sent anywhere except to TikTok's official API.

4. Sharing

We do not share, sell or transfer any user data to third parties.

5. Retention and deletion

You can disconnect at any time by revoking access in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions) and by deleting the local token file. Deleting the tool removes all data it stored.

6. Public clips

The tool processes only clips that are publicly available through the official Twitch API. Streamers can request exclusion at the contact below.
